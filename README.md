##Website Sections 

Upon entering the website, there is 5 main buttons, the learn more, volunteering, projects, goals and contact buttons. The learn more button leads to the introduction with a picture of myself, as well as two paragraphs that provides context that this website is centered around admission into physiotherapy.

The volunteering button leads to a page with images of where I am volunteering as well as a short description of what I learned when volunteering there. At the bottom of the page, there is a button that leads to a new tab that includes a reference letter I received from Peter Lougheed Center. The Projects tab leads to a page that shows the three main projects, the scratch game, kaggle, and excel. All of these are embedded and interactable, where any changes would be updated on the website after a period of time. 

The goals website is where I described why I want to go into physiotherapy and what inspired me into choosing my career. Finally, in the contacts page, there are buttons that leads to my resume, instagram and github. I was also able to include a button where upon clicking it, it would either allow the person to email me in my personal or university email depending on which button is pressed.

All of these pages have atleast one image on the top where I utilized "<img src..." so that it is not just words in each tab.
